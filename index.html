How it works

Open the file

Open the HTML file your AI tool made, from your computer or a server on your network.

Fix the words

Click and type. Ask AI to explain or suggest when you want a second opinion.

Regenerate freely

Let your AI tool rewrite the file. Your fixes come back, and its changes are highlighted for review.

You decide what AI may do

AI is optional and off until you add your own key for Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, OpenRouter, Google Gemini, or a model on your own computer. Whichever mode you choose, AI never changes your document on its own, and every change it’s part of is listed under its name.

  • Reviews and proofreads the document
  • Replies to comments
  • Suggests rewrites you can copy
  • Can’t change a word. You make every edit yourself.
Your choice

  • Everything in Read
  • Suggested rewrites get an Accept button
  • Nothing is applied until you press it
  • Each accepted change is labelled with the AI model and can be undone on its own

Questions

Does it work with Codex, Claude Code or Cursor?

Yes, with any tool that writes HTML files to your disk. Open the file in VibeRevise and leave it open while the tool works. VibeRevise checks the file every few seconds, tells you when it changes, and brings the new version in with your unsaved fixes kept.

What if the AI and I changed the same sentence?

VibeRevise won't guess. It shows both versions side by side and you pick which one to keep.

Is my document uploaded anywhere?

No. VibeRevise opens and edits your document in your browser. There are no VibeRevise servers to send it to. If you turn on AI, only the paragraphs you ask about go to the AI service you chose, when you press the button.

Which browsers does it work in?

The web app runs in any modern browser. In Chrome or Edge on a computer, Save writes straight back to the file you opened. Other browsers give you the edited copy to save yourself.

What can’t it change?

Layout, styles and images. VibeRevise changes words, and adds paragraphs, lists, tables, bold and italic like the ones already in the document. Pages built by JavaScript in the browser can’t be edited, because their text isn’t in the file.

Do I need AI to use it?

No. Every editing feature works without AI. AI is off until you add your own key.

Which AI services can I use?

Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, OpenRouter, Google Gemini, or a model on your own computer. You pay your provider directly for what you use; VibeRevise adds nothing on top.

When is the Chrome extension coming?

We’re preparing it for the Chrome Web Store. When it’s published, the button on this page will take you to it. Until then, the web app works with files on your computer or on your own network.
